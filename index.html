About Beach It

Look for the striped awning and the big yellow sun.

Beach It sits right on the Ocean City Boardwalk at 976 Boardwalk, between 9th and 10th Street. Step under the blue striped awning and you'll find racks of T-shirts, a big selection of Life is good® apparel for men, women, children and even pets, plus women's clothing, hats, accessories and gifts.

Whether you need a sweatshirt for a breezy boardwalk night, a hat for the beach, or a souvenir for someone back home, come on in and Beach It!
